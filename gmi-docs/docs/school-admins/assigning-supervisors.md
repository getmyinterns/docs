# Assigning Supervisors

Depending on your [Timesheet Settings](/school-admins/settings/timesheet-settings), `Timesheets` may need to be approved by a school supervisor, a workplace supervisor, or both. Before `Timesheets` can be processed, the relevant supervisor must be assigned in the system.

## School Supervisor

The school supervisor is assigned on the [Students details modal](/school-admins/students-details-modal).

1. Open the `Student`'s details by tapping on their name anywhere on the platform.
2. Go to the first tab and tap **_Edit_**.
3. Enter the supervisor in the **School Supervisor** field (see photo 2 on [Students Details Modal](/school-admins/students-details-modal)).
4. Save the changes.

You can also batch assign supervisors from [Reports - Students Placed](/school-admins/reports#students-placed-report) by checking multiple students and clicking on the **_Assign Supervisor_** button. This will assign the student the chosen supervisor, which will give that Admin user access to the Student as well as the ability to filter all their students they are assigned to.

## Workplace Supervisor

The workplace supervisor is assigned on the `Internship` record.

1. Go to **_Reports - Students Placed_** and tap **_Edit_** on the relevant `Internship` (last column).
2. Scroll down to the workplace supervisor section and fill in the supervisor's data (see [Internships](/school-admins/internships)).
3. Save the changes.

:::warning

If a supervisor is required by your [Timesheet Settings](/school-admins/settings/timesheet-settings) but has not been assigned, the `Timesheet` will get stuck in the approval process.

:::
